## 👥 Team Composition Rules

Your team's **Tier** affects two things:

| Rule | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Max non-0-16 players** | = Team Tier + 3 |
| **Max Rerolls** (purchasable) | = Team Tier |

> **Example:** A Tier 2 team may have up to **5** players who are not 0–16 selections, and may buy up to **2** Rerolls.
>
> ⚠️ Rerolls from the **Leader** skill and **Extra Team Training** inducements do **not** count toward the Reroll cap.

## 💰 Starting Roster & Gold

- All teams begin with **700,000 GP**.
- You may **redraft players from your own Superb Owl III roster** — but they must be the **same race/team type**.

## 🔄 Redrafting & Player Transfers

- You may buy a player carried over from Superb Owl III at an **Agent Fee of 20,000 GP per skill/advancement** the player has accumulated.
- This Agent Fee does **not** affect the player's price or the team's **Team Value (TV)**.

## ⚙️ In-Season Rules

- **No extra winnings** are awarded for not stalling.
- The spreadsheet **automatically tracks** Brawlin' Brutes (SPP from TDs and CAS).
- If your team has the **Team Captain** rule, write `Captain` before the chosen player's name — they will automatically receive the **Pro** skill.
- Skills gained during the league are chosen from the **dropdown menus** next to each player.
  - **(s)** = Selected skill | **(r)** = Random skill
  - Skills are listed alphabetically — selected first, random after.
  - Stat Increases are selected at the bottom of the list.
- Remember to fill in the number of **SPP used** in the appropriate field.
- **Players must have names** to appear on the Top 5 lists!

## 💉 Apothecary

- Apothecaries cost only **50,000 GP** (instead of the standard price).
- They work as described in the **core rulebook** (ignore the Death Zone entry).

## 📈 SPP, Advancements & Injuries

- Standard Blood Bowl rules apply for **SPP**, **Advancements**, and **Lasting Injuries**.
- **Miss Next Game** is marked with a check-mark in the appropriate field (so TV updates correctly).
- **Stat decreases** are marked in the field to the right of the player's value — so the adjustment is visible.

---

## 🎲 Rerolls

- Extra Rerolls may be purchased **during the league** at normal price (i.e. **double** what is shown on your Team Sheet) and at normal TV cost.
- **Maximum Rerolls** a team may own = the team's **Tier**.
- Rerolls from the **Leader** skill and **Extra Team Training** do **not** count toward this cap.

## 🏆 Play-Offs Format

- **Format:** Single-elimination knock-out — winners advance.
- **Seeding:** Highest-seeded teams face lowest-seeded teams each round. Seed 1 faces the lowest seed, etc.
- **Byes:** If there is an uneven number of participants, some teams receive a bye in a round.
- **Dates:**
  - Round 1: **30 April**
  - Round 2: **7 May**
- ⚠️ All participants **must show up on 30 April** to be eligible for Play-offs.
- **Locked rosters:** Lists are locked for all Play-off games. Injuries, advancements, etc. are **reset after each game**. All **Miss Next Game** statuses before Play-offs are removed.

## 📊 Scoring & Seeding

### Regular Season Points

Seeding is determined by the **best 6 results** from all weeks played.

| Result | Points |
|---|:---:|
| Win | 3 |
| Tie | 1 |
| Loss | 0 |

### Bonus League Points (up to +3 per game)

| Bonus Condition | Points |
|---|:---:|
| Score 3+ Touchdowns | +1 |
| Cause 3+ Casualties *(only those counting for SPP)* | +1 |
| Hold opponent to 0 Touchdowns | +1 |

### Tiebreakers

In case of equal points, tiebreakers are applied in this order:

1. **Win/Loss percentage**
2. **Head-to-head results**
3. **Most total SPP**

## 🎯 Kick-Off Event Table

*Used instead of the standard Blood Bowl kick-off table.*

| Roll (2D6) | Event | Effect |
|:---:|---|---|
| **2** | **Get the Ref** | Each team immediately receives one free **Bribe** Inducement. Must be used by end of game or it is lost. |
| **3** | **Time-out** | If the kicking team's turn marker is on turn 4, 5, or 6 for the half, both coaches move their marker **back** one space. Otherwise, both coaches move their marker **forward** one space. |
| **4** | **Switched Ball** | Someone switched the ball before kick-off — roll a D3 and consult the sub-table below. The result applies for the remainder of the current drive. |
| **5** | **High Kick** | One open player on the receiving team may be moved any number of squares (regardless of MA) and placed in the square where the ball will land. |
| **6** | **Cheering Fans** | Both coaches roll a D6 and add their number of Cheerleaders. The first Block Action in the highest roller's next turn receives one additional Offensive Assist. On a tie, both coaches receive this benefit. |
| **7** | **Brilliant Coaching** | Both coaches roll a D6 and add their number of Assistant Coaches. The highest total (or both on a tie) immediately gains a **free team re-roll** for this drive. Unused re-rolls are lost at end of drive. |
| **8** | **Changing Weather** | Roll on the Weather table again and apply the result. If the new result is *Perfect Conditions*, the ball scatters 3 squares in the air before landing. |
| **9** | **Quick Snap** | The receiving team's coach selects up to **D3+1 players**. Each may immediately move one square in any direction, even across the Line of Scrimmage. |
| **10** | **Charge!** | The kicking team's coach selects up to **D3+1 players**. Each may be activated for a free **Move Action**. Additionally: one may perform a free **Blitz** action, one a free **Throw Team-mate** action, and one a free **Kick Team-mate** action. If a selected player Falls Over or is Knocked Down, all remaining activations end immediately. |
| **11** | **Dodgy Snack** | Both coaches roll a D6. The lowest roller (or both on a tie) randomly selects one player on the pitch and rolls a D6: on **2+**, that player's pre-drive snack disagreed with them — reduce their MA and AV by 1 for this drive. On a **1**, the player is placed in the Reserves box for the rest of the drive! |
| **12** | **Pitch Invasion** | Both coaches roll a D6 and add their Fan Factor. The lowest roller (or both on a tie) randomly selects **D3 of their players on the pitch**. Selected players are immediately Placed Prone and become Stunned. |

### Switched Ball Sub-Table (Roll D3)

| D3 | Ball Type | Effect |
|:---:|---|---|
| **1** | **Rock Hewn Ball** | Ball only scatters D3 squares on kick-off. The carrier gains the **Juggernaut** skill but loses 1 MA. Ball can only be thrown for Quick and Short passes. |
| **2** | **Floating Ball** | +1 modifier to all throw attempts. However, +1 modifier to all interception attempts as well. |
| **3** | **Sticky Ball** | +1 modifier to pick-up and catch attempts. −1 modifier to pass and hand-off attempts. |

## 🙏 Desperate Prayers to Nuffles Measures

*Replaces both Desperate Measures and Prayers to Nuffle. Purchased as an inducement (0–3, 50,000 GP each).*

| Roll (D8) | Name | Effect |
|:---:|---|---|
| **1** | **You Dope** | Play during Step 1 of Start of Drive. Choose one player — improve their **STR or AG by 1**, or **PA by 2** for this game. When a drive they participated in ends, roll a D6: on **3+** they feel great; on **1–2** they suffer a terrible allergic reaction and must miss the rest of the match. |
| **2** | **Razzle-Dazzle** | Play when activating a player. That player may perform **two different actions** this activation (not the same action twice, and not two actions that both include a Move action). |
| **3** | **Hangover** | Play before Step 1 of Start of Drive. Choose an opposing player — they **miss the next drive**, even if this means the opponent fields fewer than 7 players. |
| **4** | **Turn the Blind Eye** | Declare before one of your team turns. Players **cannot be sent off for Fouling** during that turn. |
| **5** | **Set Piece** | Play when activating a player to perform a **Pass action**. The pass is **automatically accurate** and (unless intercepted) the ball is **automatically caught**. |
| **6** | **Sports Espionage** | Play when your team suffers a **Turnover**. Your team immediately gains a **free team re-roll**. |
| **7** | **Timewarp Training** | Play during Step 1 of Start of Drive. Choose one player on your team — they may choose a **Secondary Skill** for the duration of this game. |
| **8** | **Potion of Many Things** | Play during Step 1 of Start of Drive. Choose one player on your team — they may choose a **Mutation** for the duration of this game. |

## 🛠️ Sheet Setup Guide

### Creating a New Team

1. **Find the Master Sheet** and duplicate it.
2. **Rename** the duplicated sheet to your desired team name.
3. In the **Liga Overblik** sheet, go to column C and **insert a link** to your new sheet:
   *(Insert → Link → Sheet and Named Ranges → Find your sheet)*
4. If done correctly, all data will now pull automatically from your sheet.

### Troubleshooting

**Q: My link is no longer working!**
> A: You likely renamed your sheet after creating the link. Delete the link and create a new one.

**Q: My team name isn't shown in blue and there are errors in the overview.**
> A: You probably typed the sheet name in column C instead of inserting a link. See step 3 above.
